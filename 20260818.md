# Tuesday, August 18, 2026

**Agent sprawl is the new SaaS sprawl.** McKinsey estimates that Agentic AI builds could add US$2.6 to $4.4 trillion annually to the world economy. However, the rush to create new value can lead to agent sprawl, which occurs when AI agents are created, deployed, or connected across systems faster than the enterprise can inventory them, assign ownership, control permissions, monitor behavior, and optimize or retire them when they are no longer fit for purpose. Uncoordinated agentic deployments have become an enterprise governance risk, not an IT risk ([SAP News Center](https://news.sap.com/2026/08/agent-sprawl-why-ai-governance-is-now-board-level-issue)). Gartner projects the average Fortune 500 will run more than 150,000 AI agents by 2028, but only 13% believe they have adequate governance today. Separately, Gartner warns 40%+ of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027, with integration complexity and governance failure (not model capability) as primary causes ([TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322608/20260801/nc-ai-lands-koreas-34m-agentic-enterprise-mandate-gabia-live-testbed.htm)). Mid-market companies who never solved SaaS governance are the highest-risk cohort here. 

**AI workforce cuts are generating buyer's remorse and a rehire cycle.** Forrester reports 55% of employers who cut headcount citing AI already regret the decision; Gartner estimates 50% will rehire in similar functions under different titles by 2027 ([Spiceworks](https://www.spiceworks.com/it-careers/why-companies-are-hiring-workers-back-after-ai-layoffs)). Ford's case is the named example: 300 veteran QA inspectors rehired after AI "didn't live up to quality expectations." At the macro level, tech sector job cuts through June 2026 reached 139,156 with AI as the most-cited cause for four straight months ([IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/us-employers-announce-august-layoffs-1812019)). 

**A vendor-neutral AI ROI standard is being built.** The Linux Foundation launched the Tokenomics Foundation on August 4 with 30 founding members including Accenture, IBM, Oracle, SAP, ServiceNow, JPMorganChase, and BNY ([Linux Foundation / PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/linux-foundation-launches-the-tokenomics-foundation-to-define-the-economics-and-roi-of-ai-value-302842344.html)). The roadmap includes open AI Value Frameworks, a vendor-neutral total cost of AI reference model, and a "cost to serve" standard (cost per call, not cost per token). If the member list is any signal, this will become the baseline methodology auditors and procurement teams cite within 18 months. Firms with early literacy will have a structural advantage in governance and measurement engagements.
